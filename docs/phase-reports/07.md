## Decisions and limits

The corpus is authored educational material, not actual production incidents or a source of episode truth. Generic runbooks remain usable before January 2026; the six historical notes are only eligible after their February publication. Unknown publication time is excluded from projection. Source-available replay refuses the synthetic trace/document inputs because trustworthy availability timestamps are absent. Assertions are explicit document claims and cannot write telemetry edges. Two fuzzy `order` mentions remain unresolved in review. The annotation set is author-reviewed and small; independent human review remains pending before external release. No embeddings or retrieval quality are claimed until Phase 08. The offline leakage scan checks exact private identifiers and field names but cannot establish semantic non-leakage alone.
